About the Firm

Dattatri M. Benaknalli
Senior Advocate

Dattatri MB & Co. is one of Bidar's most respected law firms, with over six years of dedicated practice in Civil, Criminal, Family, Real Estate, Corporate, and Matrimonial law. The firm holds strong expertise in JMFC and CJM Court proceedings in Bidar, along with District & Sessions Court litigations and Karnataka High Court matters.

Fully updated on India's landmark criminal law reforms — the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) which came into force on July 1, 2024 — the firm delivers modern, informed legal strategies alongside time-tested courtroom advocacy. We are committed to swift resolutions, transparent client communication, and ethical legal practice at every step.
